# Global instructions (all projects)

## Lesson capture

After any of these, propose capturing a one-line lesson with /learn before moving on:

- the user corrects you — a REPEAT correction means an existing lesson failed to fire: propose a REWRITE, never a duplicate
- an assumption you held turned out wrong, or a fix took more than one attempt (the root cause is the lesson)
- a non-obvious workaround was found

Lessons are per-project (`.claude/rules/lessons.md`). /learn handles routing, dedup, and approval — here, only notice and propose.
